Privacy Policy

Micro: Money, Body, Mind · Effective October 18, 2026

The short version: Micro keeps your data on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We don’t receive your habit logs, your notes, or any personal information.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Micro: Money, Body, Mind mobile app (“Micro” or “the app”).

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Habit logs: the dates you log a Money, Body, or Mind habit, and the amount for Money entries.
  • Optional Mind check-in details: a mood emoji and a short note, only if you choose to add them.
  • Preferences: your appearance setting (light, dark, or system), which habits you track, your monthly targets, your reminder choice and time, whether you finished onboarding, and whether Premium features are unlocked.

All of this lives in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any server.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. Micro has no sign-in and no accounts. It does not show ads, and it does not include analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we never receive it. Micro does not include third-party tools that collect data about you. Google Play, the store you use to install the app, may collect information under its own privacy policy. That is separate from this app.

5. Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing about your reminders is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s notification settings.

6. Purchases

Premium features may be offered as an in-app purchase or subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details.

7. Device backups

Your phone’s operating system may back up app data, for example Android’s backup to your Google account, depending on your device settings. That is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

You can erase your logged entries and habit settings at any time with Settings → Reset all data inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes the rest of the data it stores. Because your data stays on your device, we can’t access or recover it for you.

9. Children

Micro is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app collects any new kind of data, we will update this policy before that version is released.
